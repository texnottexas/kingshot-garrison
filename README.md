# How 2 Garrison - A Kingshot Fortress Codex

A modern, single-page redesign of a community **Kingshot** garrison defense guide: how to join a
garrison, troop ratios, the only joiner skill that matters, defensive heroes, reading battle reports,
remedies, forts and sanctuaries, castle battle tactics, batch healing, and R4/R5 garrison management.

**Live page:** https://texnottexas.github.io/kingshot-garrison/

Comes in two versions, switchable from the toggle at the top of either page:
- **Codex** (`index.html`) - a consistent, calm read in a fortress-parchment serif style.
- **Field Manual** (`v2.html`) - a bold, per-section "war room" treatment.
